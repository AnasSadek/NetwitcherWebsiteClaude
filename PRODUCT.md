## Platform

## Users

Primary buyers are decision-makers at small and mid-sized businesses across the
DACH region who want more qualified inquiries but have no in-house content or
marketing team. Confirmed segments, all treated as primary:

- **Lokale Berliner KMU** — restaurants, beauty, Handwerk, local service providers.
- **E-Commerce & Produktmarken** — online shops and DTC brands needing product
  photography, reels, and ads.
- **B2B & Mittelstand** — service firms with longer sales cycles, lead-focused.
- **Gründer & Start-ups** — new companies needing brand, website, and first content.

Typical situation: an owner or marketing lead comparing agencies — often on a
phone — who needs proof the agency will produce *inquiries*, not just posts.
Their job to be done: turn attention into booked calls and paying customers.

## Product Purpose

Netwitcher is a Berlin-based digital agency **and** content studio. It exists to
turn attention into inquiries: it both produces the content (product photography,
reels, video, social content) and runs the campaigns and websites that convert
it — from production through performance, in one team. Success for a visitor is a
completed conversion action: a booked Erstgespräch, a requested content/marketing
package, or a WhatsApp contact.

## Positioning

The ownable mechanism a neighboring agency cannot truthfully copy: content
production and performance marketing **from one hand**, backed by Netwitcher's
**own physical content studio in Berlin** (not outsourced), with genuine
multicultural/multilingual understanding of audiences in the German market. Most
competitors either only make pretty content or only run ads; Netwitcher owns the
full "attention → conversion" chain and the studio behind it. Brand narrative:
*Aus Aufmerksamkeit werden Anfragen.*

## Product Principles

1. **Attention is only the start.** Every asset — photo, reel, ad, page — must
   move the visitor toward an inquiry, not just impressions.
2. **Production and performance belong together.** The team that makes the content
   also runs the campaigns and site that convert it; that unity is the offer.
3. **The Berlin studio is a real, ownable advantage** — proximity, speed, and
   consistent quality that outsourced content can't match.
4. **Serve a broad SMB spectrum without diluting.** The offer flexes across local
   KMU, e-commerce, B2B, and founders; positioning stays specific per audience.
5. **Honesty over hype.** No fabricated proof. Real numbers replace placeholders
   only when the owner supplies them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- German-language audience; multicultural/multilingual audience understanding is a
  positioning asset, not just a service.
- Target standard: WCAG AA. The 3D/animated experiences must keep their existing
  `prefers-reduced-motion` and no-WebGL fallbacks, and remain fully usable without
  the animation layer.
